# SlapPhone Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

## Overview
SlapPhone is a simple app that detects phone slaps and plays sounds.

## Data Collection
SlapPhone does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Permissions
- **Accelerometer:** Used to detect phone slaps. Data is processed locally and never stored or shared.
- **Foreground Service:** Keeps the app running in the background. No data is collected.

## Third Parties
SlapPhone does not share any data with third parties.
